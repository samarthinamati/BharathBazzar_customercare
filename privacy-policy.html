Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bharat Bazzar (“we”, “our” or “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Bharat Bazzar mobile application, website and related marketplace services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name, phone number, email address and account/profile details.
  • Delivery and billing information such as recipient name, phone number, address, city, state and PIN code.
  • Product, cart, order, return, refund and order-status information.
  • Seller information such as store name, owner/contact details, business information, product information and pickup/warehouse details.
  • Messages, support requests and other communications you send through the app or support channels.
  • Payment transaction references, payment method and payment status. Payment credentials such as UPI PINs, card PINs and banking passwords are not requested by Bharat Bazzar.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the app, such as device, browser, app and diagnostic information where applicable.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts; display and sell products; process orders; coordinate customers and sellers; arrange delivery and pickup; process returns and refunds; facilitate payments; provide customer and seller support; send service-related updates; prevent fraud and misuse; maintain app security; and improve Bharat Bazzar and its marketplace services.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers, including Razorpay. Payment processing is subject to the provider’s terms and privacy policy. Bharat Bazzar may receive transaction references and payment status needed to confirm an order, payment, refund or other transaction. Do not share your UPI PIN, card PIN, banking password or OTP with anyone claiming to represent Bharat Bazzar.

4. Delivery, Sellers and Marketplace Sharing

Bharat Bazzar is a marketplace. To fulfil an order, relevant information may be shared with the seller responsible for the product and with delivery, logistics or pickup partners. This may include the information necessary to prepare, collect, deliver or return an order, such as recipient name, delivery address, phone number, product/order details and delivery instructions.

Seller information may be used to support marketplace operations, product fulfilment, pickup and seller account management.

5. When We Share Information

We share information only as reasonably necessary for operating the service, including with:

  • Sellers to fulfil products and marketplace orders.
  • Courier, logistics and delivery partners to deliver or return orders.
  • Payment providers to process and verify transactions.
  • Hosting, database, cloud, communication, analytics and technology providers that help operate Bharat Bazzar.
  • Customer-support providers where needed to resolve requests.
  • Government authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law or legal process.

We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Storage, Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, complete transactions, handle returns or disputes, maintain records, meet legal or accounting requirements and enforce applicable agreements.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or related services may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, preferences, security, analytics or service improvement. You can manage certain cookie or browser permissions through your device or browser settings.

8. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law and legitimate business or legal requirements. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Children's Privacy

Bharat Bazzar is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

10. Third-Party Services

Bharat Bazzar may rely on third-party services for payment processing, hosting, cloud storage, communication, analytics and delivery. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the relevant provider policies where applicable.

11. Data Security and Account Responsibility

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for notifying us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization. Bharat Bazzar will not ask you to disclose your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or banking password through an unsolicited message or phone call.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of Bharat Bazzar after an update means the updated policy applies to your use of the services, subject to applicable law.
